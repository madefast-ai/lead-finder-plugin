# Writing outreach

Drafts only. The user reviews and sends every message themselves.

## Every first message

- **One specific, true hook** from the row: their post, comment, a hire, a launch, their Reddit question. Never invent or guess.
- **One ask**, small and easy to answer.
- **Where their details came from and how to opt out** (GDPR, Art. 14). Short versions:
  - LinkedIn note or message: "Found your profile on LinkedIn. If you'd rather not hear from me, just say so."
  - Email footer: "I found your details on LinkedIn, where you shared them publicly. Not interested? Reply 'no' and I'll delete them. [Sender, company, address]"
  - Reddit or Instagram DM: "Saw your post in r/<sub> / on Instagram. Say the word and I won't message again."
  Set `data_source_note_sent=yes` when the draft includes it.
- Language and formality from `offer.md`. Use `{{salutation}}` only when the profile makes the right form clear; otherwise the full name.

## Limits

| Channel | Limit |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn connection note | 200 characters (free LinkedIn), 300 (Premium). No link, no pitch: the only goal is the accepted invite. |
| Message after connecting | 50-90 words, one question |
| InMail | subject under 8 words, 60-120 words |
| Cold email | subject under 7 words; 60-110 words (100-150 for a free-report email); plain text; footer with sender identity and opt-out |
| Follow-up | under 60 words, one new useful detail |
| Reddit public reply | answer the question first, genuinely; mention what you do only if it helps and the subreddit allows it; no links unless asked |
| Reddit / Instagram DM | under 70 words, reference their post, no hard pitch |

## Email consent by country

Cold email to businesses needs **prior consent** in some countries, for example **Romania** (Law 506/2004 art. 12, also for companies) and **Germany** (UWG §7). Before drafting an email, check the lead's location. For those countries, warn the user and suggest LinkedIn instead. The UK, Ireland, the Netherlands, Belgium and France are more permissive for B2B (opt-out), but the data-source note and an opt-out are always required.

## Platform etiquette

- LinkedIn: connect first with a short note; message after acceptance. Don't send the same text to many people at the same company; check the workbook for colleagues already contacted and coordinate.
- Reddit: many subreddits ban self-promotion; a helpful public reply beats a DM. Never DM someone who only commented, unless they asked for recommendations.
- Instagram: businesses often prefer DMs; comment only when you add something.

## Writing style

- Plain, specific, human. Short sentences. No buzzwords ("streamline", "leverage", "unlock", "game-changer", "synergy").
- Never use an em dash or en dash as punctuation: use a period, a comma or a plain hyphen.
- No "it's not X, it's Y" pivots, no "I hope this finds you well", no "just bumping this", no fake familiarity.
- Numbers only from `offer.md` proof or a saved report. Never invent results.
- One idea per message.
